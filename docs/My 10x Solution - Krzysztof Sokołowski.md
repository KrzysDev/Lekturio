# "Lekturio" - My 10x Solution

## The problem

Polish secondary school students are often given assignments related to 
required reading ("lektury"). As a former high school student, I know 
how difficult it can be to find specific information inside a book — 
especially when it's just a small detail that no one really talks about. 
That's what Lekturio is here for: a 10x easier way to search for 
information inside Polish set books ("lektury").

## Predictions

Looking for certain in books is a long process. You have to either read it (the longest way) or find information online from summaries (it takes around 15 minutes to read long summary of a book)
I belive, when i finish the system the user will be able to find certain information inside the book in less than a 30 seconds. By then it's not 10x project - it is 30x.
